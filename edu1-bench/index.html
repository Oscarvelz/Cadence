edu1 bench

Timing measurement for the edu1 int8 artifact on this computer, in this browser. The model ships in the external-data layout (25 files, the largest 24,674,304 bytes; 276,103,599 bytes in total). Generation runs a page-side beam search over the raw ONNX sessions under the bench decoding (10 beams, 5 candidates, repetition penalty 2.0, no-repeat-ngram 2, max length 320), verified against the parity record.

The counted presses are edu1's 15 unique paragraphs from the development bank, after one uncounted warm-up press. The verdict is the share of presses that miss 10 seconds, counted across all presses and on the 15 alone. A press over 10 s on a stronger machine is over 10 s on a weaker one; a press under 10 s here proves nothing for a weaker machine.

On load, this page first checks whether the model is already in Cache Storage (that check is the browser-restart survival probe: open this page, run the measurement, close the browser completely, reopen the page, and read the cache line again).
